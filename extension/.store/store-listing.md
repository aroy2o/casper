## Detailed description (16,000 chars max)

CASPER (Corruption Analysis & Price Scrutiny Engine for Regulations) is an AI-powered browser extension that automatically detects price inflation, collusion patterns, and procurement irregularities in government tender documents — as you read them.

### How it works

1. Open any government tender page (eProcure, GeM, eTenders, state portals, World Bank, etc.)
2. The CASPER button appears automatically when a tender page is detected
3. Click "Audit Tender" — CASPER extracts the document text and runs an ML audit
4. Receive an instant report: risk level, flagged line items, inflation percentages, and recommendations

### Features

• **Real-time analysis** — No need to upload files. CASPER reads the page directly.
• **AI-powered verdict** — Detects overpriced line items by comparing quoted rates against market references.
• **Risk scoring** — LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / CRITICAL rating based on overall inflation and fraud signals.
• **Flagged line items** — Each suspicious item shows: quoted rate vs. market rate, % inflation, confidence score, and explanation.
• **Audit history** — All past analyses are stored and accessible in your CASPER dashboard.
• **Works on major portals** — eProcure.gov.in, GeM.gov.in, eTenders, and any public procurement website.

### Who is it for?

- Journalists and RTI activists investigating tender fraud
- Government auditors and vigilance officers
- Civil society organisations tracking procurement transparency
- Researchers studying public procurement

### Privacy

CASPER only reads page text when you explicitly click "Audit Tender". It connects to your self-hosted CASPER backend (required). No data is sent to third-party servers.

### Setup

1. Install the extension
2. Click the CASPER icon → Settings
3. Paste your CASPER JWT access token (from your CASPER dashboard → Settings → API Token)
4. Set your backend URL (default: http://localhost:4000 for local dev)
5. Browse any tender page and click "Audit Tender"
